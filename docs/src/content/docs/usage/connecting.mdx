## Home screen

From top to bottom, the home screen shows:

- **Profile card** — the subscription logo and name, the update button and the provider's announcement.
- **Subscription stats** — **Traffic left**, **Days left** and **Expires**. The ∞ sign means unlimited
  traffic or a subscription that never expires.
- **Connect button** — the status (**Disconnected**, **Connecting...**, **Connected**), the session
  time and its traffic.
- **Current server** — the selection in the first proxy group. Click it to open the
  [proxy groups](../proxies/).
- **Support** — the provider's support link, if it sent one.

Three days before the subscription ends, the stats are replaced by a warning. If the provider sent a
payment link, a renewal button appears under it.

## Connect button

The big button turns proxying on and off. What exactly it turns on is set in **Settings** →
**Main Switch**:

| Mode | How it works | When to choose it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **TUN Mode** (default) | Creates a virtual network interface and captures the traffic of all apps, UDP included. | Games, messengers, programs without proxy settings — almost always. |
| **Proxy Mode** | Sets the system proxy. Browsers and programs that respect it go through it. | When TUN is not available, for example without administrator rights. |

<Aside>
  TUN needs permissions: on Windows, one launch as administrator; on macOS and Linux, authorizing the
  core. See [Installation](../../introduction/installation/) for details.
</Aside>

## Outbound mode

The bottom of the side menu has a mode switch:

- **Rules** — every connection goes where the subscription's [rules](../rules/) send it: through the
  proxy, directly or blocked. This is the normal mode.
- **Global** — all traffic goes through the server selected in the `GLOBAL` group.

The provider can forbid global mode. Then the switch is hidden and the profile always works by rules.

## Tray and floating window

When you close the window, Koala Clash keeps running in the tray. From the tray menu you can:

- show the window;
- turn the connection on or off;
- change the outbound mode and the [profile](../subscriptions/#several-profiles);
- open the app folders, for example the **Log Directory**;
- quit the app.

The floating window is a small indicator on top of other windows; turn it on in the
[appearance settings](../settings/#appearance). Clicking it opens the main window, right-clicking
opens the tray menu.

## Quitting

Quit completely from the tray (**Quit App**) or with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Q</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Q</kbd>
on macOS). The app asks for confirmation; to quit right away, press the shortcut twice or hold it.
Proxying stops after quitting.
